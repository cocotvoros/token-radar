How the radar prices a launch

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth.

  • Category multiple: the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the protocols in that category whose token already trades, shrunk toward the global median: (n × category median + k × global median) / (n + k), so a category priced by one or two thin protocols cannot set its own price. Each live ratio is capped at 10× first; the global median is used when a category has no live protocol. The shrinkage k is chosen on the leave-one-out grid below, not by taste.
  • Range: the same formula with the category's first and third quartiles of capped live ratios, so every row shows a low–high range instead of false precision.
  • Size curve: a log–log fit of market cap on TVL over the 128 live-token protocols, drawn on the board's scatter as a cross-check. It never prices a row: TVL alone leaves most of the variance unexplained, and the detail view shows what the curve alone would say next to the board's own value.
  • Growth: the protocol's 7-day TVL change, clamped to −40%…+120%, weighted 0.35.
  • Maturity: 0.80 for a brand-new listing, rising to 1.20 after four years tracked with no token; 0.85 when no listing date is recorded.
  • Breadth: +3% per extra chain, up to five.

Farm economics, netted

Every plan is costed for one standard farmer: $1,000 deployed for 4 weeks. Cost is $6 setup + $4 exit + 4 weeks of the category's weekly transactions at the main chain's gas (Ethereum $4.50, BSC $0.30, major L2s $0.15, Solana and Polygon $0.05, other chains $0.50). The expected airdrop assumes a launch sets aside 5% of the implied value for its farmers, split pro-rata to capital, and is shown per $1K deployed. Verdict: FARM when the net is positive even at the range's low end, THIN when only the point estimate nets positive, SKIP when even that does not cover the gas. The airdrop share and the pro-rata split are this board's stated assumptions, not a promise: a launch may set aside more, less, or nothing.

The whole snapshot, accounted for

A protocol counts as pre-token when the snapshot records no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol for it, and it is not a versioned, wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades. The board holds every such protocol — the 40 on the house watchlist are the floor, never the ceiling.

Every row carries a six-step farm plan, written for its own category, with what each step costs and how long it takes; the last step nets the plan's own economics. An implied value is this board's own claim about what a launch would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. It is not a prediction that a token will be issued, and not financial advice.
